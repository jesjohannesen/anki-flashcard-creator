Welcome to Anki Flashcard Creator

Highlight any text → AI suggests a flashcard → Save to Anki.

1 Install AnkiConnect in Anki

AnkiConnect is the bridge between your browser and Anki. In Anki desktop: Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, paste the code 2055492159, then restart Anki.

2 Connect this extension to Anki

This grants the extension permission to save cards to Anki. Make sure Anki is open, then click below — a popup will appear inside Anki asking you to approve. Click Yes.

3 Pick your AI provider

How should we generate flashcard suggestions from your highlights?

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey.
Requires Chrome 138+, ~22GB free disk, GPU with 4GB+ VRAM. Enable both: chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano and chrome://flags/#optimization-guide-on-device-model, then restart Chrome. First card generation will download a ~2GB model.

4 Preferences (optional)

Keyboard shortcut: ⌘⇧A (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+A (Win/Linux). Rebind at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

5 Try it

Open any article. Highlight a sentence. Right-click → "Create Anki flashcard from selection", or use the keyboard shortcut, or click the floating "+" button.

Open settings
Your highlighted text is sent to your chosen AI provider for card generation. See the privacy policy for details.
