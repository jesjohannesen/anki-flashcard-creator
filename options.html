Anki Flashcard Creator

Settings sync across your Chrome profile. Re-run welcome / setup.

AI provider

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. The free tier has daily rate limits — if you hit them often, switch to Chrome Built-in above.

Anki note type

Default Basic works out of the box. Must have Front and Back fields.

Quick capture

Keyboard shortcut: ⌘⇧A on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+A on Windows/Linux. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Setup checklist

  • Install AnkiConnect in Anki (Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons → 2055492159), then restart Anki.
  • In AnkiConnect's config, add your extension origin to webCorsOriginList. Your extension origin is shown below — copy it into the list.
  • Keep Anki desktop open while saving cards. They sync to AnkiWeb/mobile normally.
